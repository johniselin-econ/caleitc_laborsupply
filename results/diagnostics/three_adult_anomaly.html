The Three-Adult Anomaly

The paper's headline heterogeneity result puts the entire full-time decline in households with three or more adults. Under the fourth difference it disappears, and the college placebo shows the same pattern in a group that cannot be responding to the credit.

Reading the panels

The pooled column behaves exactly as the paper describes. In the all-households panel the triple difference is flat before 2015 and breaks cleanly after. The part-time placebo is a precise null in every year, which is why the part-time headline survives the fourth difference. Nothing here is in question.

The three-adult column is a different object. In the part-time placebo, college-educated women in large California households sit five to eight points above their control-state counterparts in every year, including 2012 and 2013. That is not a treatment effect. It is a statement that the cell is not comparable across states to begin with, and the level gap is roughly the size of the effect the paper attributes to the credit.

Cutting by household size destabilizes every design, not just one. Counting panels that fail a pre-period check, the three-plus cell fails four of six and the two-adult cell also fails four of six, while the pooled column fails one of six. The single pooled failure is the quadruple difference on part-time, whose 2013 coefficient carries a t-statistic of 2.61. That is a caveat on the part-time headline and worth carrying forward, though the effect is large and persistent enough after 2015 to survive it.

The full-time picture in that cell is unstable in both directions. The triple difference shows a large post-2015 decline but starts from a significantly positive 2012 coefficient. The placebo swings from −8.9 in 2013 to −10.1 in 2017 with no break at the policy date. The quadruple difference wanders positive throughout with no discontinuity at all.

The robust conclusion

The full-time decline in three-plus adult households does not survive the fourth difference, and the cell fails the placebo test that the pooled sample passes.

Both quadruple-difference implementations agree on this, the subsample version at +0.55 and the pooled interaction at +4.56. The cell-level magnitudes are not stable enough to interpret on their own, but the direction of the failure is not in doubt.

The argument against this reading

The college placebo may not be a clean control in exactly this cell.

Treatment is assigned on the woman's own education, but the credit is claimed on a tax unit inside a household. In a three-adult household, the other adults may themselves be CalEITC-eligible even when she is not. If so, the control group is partly treated, the fourth difference subtracts a genuine household-level response, and the quadruple difference over-differences precisely where the paper's mechanism operates. This is the one story under which the heterogeneity is real, and it is testable. See mechanism 5.

What could be happening in California, concentrated in large households

Any candidate has to clear four hurdles at once. It has to be specific to California, since state-by-year fixed effects absorb everything national. It has to arrive around 2015. It has to bite harder where a woman has qualifying children. And it has to reach college-educated women too, because they show it. Ordered by how well each fits that pattern.

Specifications. Sample is single women aged 20 to 49, US citizens, not in school, not potential qualifying children, in California and control states, 2012 to 2017, weighted, from the American Community Survey. Full-time is 35 or more hours per week. The triple difference and placebo use the paper's event fixed effects with demographic controls and state unemployment and minimum wage interacted with qualifying-child count. The quadruple difference uses the saturated three-way fixed effects from the existing quad-diff specification, with the education dimension as the fourth difference, estimated within each household-size cell.

Validation. The pooled triple-difference and quadruple-difference estimates reproduce the committed tables exactly before any new cut was taken.
